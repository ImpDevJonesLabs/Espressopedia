# Privacy Policy for Espressopedia

**Last Updated:** November 6, 2026  
**Effective Date:** November 6, 2026  

**Espressopedia** ("we," "our," or "the App"), developed by **JonesLabs13**, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you use the Espressopedia mobile application for Android.

By downloading and using Espressopedia, you agree to the practices outlined in this policy.

---

### 1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

#### A. Information You Provide Directly
Espressopedia does not require user registration, account creation, or logins. We **do not** collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, or payment details.

#### B. Data Stored Locally on Your Device
The App stores basic configuration preferences locally on your device via Android's internal storage. This data never leaves your device and includes:
* Default brew timer duration
* Custom coffee-to-water baseline ratios
* Selected notification ringtone/alarm sound URI
* App theme preference (Light, Dark, or System Default)

You can erase this data at any time by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App from your device settings.

---

### 2. Device Permissions Used

Espressopedia requests only the minimum device permissions necessary to provide its core features:

| Permission | Technical Name | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vibration & Haptics** | `android.permission.VIBRATE` | Used solely to deliver tactile haptic feedback during phase transitions in the multi-phase brew timer and button clicks. |
| **Network & Internet** | `android.permission.INTERNET`, `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` | Required and utilized by Google AdMob to load native advertisements within the brew guide section. The core timer and calculator functions operate fully offline. |

Espressopedia **does not** request or access:
* Precise or approximate GPS location
* Device camera or microphone
* Contacts, calendars, or call logs
* External shared storage / photos / media files

---

### 3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Espressopedia displays native banner/in-feed advertisements through **Google AdMob** to support ongoing development. 

Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous device data, including:
* Android Advertising ID (AAID)
* IP address (used for approximate geographic location and fraud detection)
* Device model, operating system version, and mobile network carrier
* Interaction and performance metrics (ad impressions, clicks, diagnostics)

For more information on how Google processes and safeguards data, please review:
* [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
* [Google AdMob Privacy & Security](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543)

#### Opting Out of Interest-Based Advertising
You can reset your advertising identifier or opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your Android device settings:
> **Settings** → **Google** → **Ads** → **Delete advertising ID** (or **Opt out of Ads Personalization** on older Android versions).

### 4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Espressopedia is designed for general audiences and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate measures.

---

### 5. Data Retention & Security

Because we do not operate external servers or user databases, we do not retain or transmit your personal data. Any preferences stored on your device are protected under standard Android operating system app-sandboxing boundaries.

---

### 6. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA / CPRA)

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as the European Economic Area or California), you have rights regarding your personal data:
* **Right to Access / Deletion:** Because all user preferences are stored locally on your device, you have complete control over them. You may erase all locally stored data at any time by navigating to **Settings → Apps → Espressopedia → Storage → Clear Data**.
* **Right to Opt-Out of Sale / Sharing:** We do not sell user personal data. To manage third-party ad tracking, use the device advertising controls described in Section 3.
